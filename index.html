Voltic Partaker Backup

Voltic Partaker Backup is a private, owner-operated application designed to transfer onboard camera, received-radio, radar, vessel-contact, and telemetry recordings to the owner's Google Drive account.

Privacy policyOperating terms

Privacy policy

The application requests access only to Google Drive files it creates or files explicitly selected for it. It does not request access to Gmail or the entire contents of Google Drive.

Backup files contain recordings, timestamps, manifests, and integrity checksums. These files are stored privately in the owner's Google Drive account. The application does not sell this data, use it for advertising, or deliberately share it with outside recipients.

Google authorization credentials remain on the owner's onboard computer in restricted-access files. The owner can revoke authorization through their Google Account settings.

Cloud recordings are not automatically deleted. Local recordings older than 24 hours may be removed only after the corresponding cloud copy has passed integrity verification. Unverified recordings are retained locally.

The owner may enable browser notifications for backup problems. Notifications contain storage, backlog or error status, not recordings. Device push-subscription credentials are stored privately on the onboard computer. Notifications are delivered through the browser's push provider to the owner's opted-in device.

Operating terms

This application is for the owner's private recording archive, not a public backup service. The owner controls its configuration, Google authorization, and cloud files. The application does not control vessel navigation or sensor operation.
